## Project Overview

The Lounge at Lodi Crush came to us locked into an industry-specific CMS that made even small updates slow and required a developer for anything beyond swapping text. We rebuilt their entire web presence on Astro JS, giving them a fast, fully-owned platform designed to match the warmth and character of the physical space.

### Key Features

- Full migration off a locked-down industry CMS to a self-hosted Astro JS site
- Custom design system reflecting the lounge's warm, tactile brand identity
- Editable content structure the client can update without a developer
- Responsive layout optimized for mobile browsing and reservations
- Fast, static-first performance with no vendor lock-in

### Approach

We worked closely with the team at The Lounge at Lodi Crush to translate the in-person feel of the space — warm lighting, wine, and conversation — into a digital experience, while giving them full ownership over their own content going forward.

### Results

The migration eliminated recurring CMS licensing costs, gave the client independent control over their content, and delivered a site that better represents the brand's character online.
