## Project Overview

Central Valley Cancer Foundation needed a website that felt more current and was easier for visitors to navigate, without disrupting how the facility's own staff manage day-to-day content. Rather than migrating them to a new platform, we kept Wix — the CMS the facility and its workers were already comfortable using — and focused the redesign on modernizing the look and improving usability.

### Key Deliverables

- Redesigned site structure across all core sections — About Us, Cancer Info, Events, Blog and News, Get Involved, Donate, Apply, and Contact — built and managed in Wix
- Streamlined financial-assistance application flow with clear eligibility guidelines and a downloadable PDF grant application
- Simplified donation experience with a direct donation link and QR code for on-the-go giving
- Refreshed visual identity — updated typography, imagery, and a clearer presentation of the foundation's mission and board of directors
- Contact form giving patients, volunteers, and donors a direct line to the foundation

### Approach

We worked within Wix's existing tooling so the foundation's team could keep managing the site day-to-day without retraining, putting our effort into modernizing the visual design and improving the experience for visitors.

### Results

- Faster page load times across the site
- Fully mobile-friendly experience
- Improved accessibility for clients across the board
