## Project Overview

FuelerLinx runs corporate aviation's largest jet fuel marketplace, handling over $3.2B in annual transactions, 120,000+ monthly dispatches, and 5,000+ aircraft. Their public marketing site hadn't been touched since 2017 and no longer reflected the scale or authority of the platform. We rebuilt it from the ground up.

### Key Deliverables

- Information architecture restructured around enterprise buyer workflows
- Navigation redesigned by audience segment: Platform, Capabilities, Integrations, Mobile, Solutions, and DOD & Warfighter
- Shift from a generic, dated look to a dark, authoritative aesthetic
- Real product UI integrated in place of stock photography
- Mobile-first, fully responsive design across all breakpoints
- Accessibility considered from the initial design phase, not bolted on after

### Results

- 75% increase in average session length
- 80% improvement in device performance and load times
- 100% mobile-first responsiveness across the site
