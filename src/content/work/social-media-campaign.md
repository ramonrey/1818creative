## Project Overview

GreenEarth Initiative needed to raise awareness about environmental sustainability. We created a viral campaign that resonated with their target audience.

### Campaign Strategy

- Audience research and segmentation
- Content calendar development
- Influencer partnerships
- User-generated content initiatives
- Real-time engagement monitoring

### Campaign Highlights

- Reached 1.2 million users organically
- 85,000+ engagements across platforms
- 25 influencer partnerships
- 15,000 pieces of user-generated content
- Featured in major media outlets

### Impact

The campaign not only met its awareness goals but also drove a 300% increase in website traffic and significant growth in newsletter subscriptions.
