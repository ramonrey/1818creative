## Project Overview

FitLife approached us to design a mobile app that would make fitness tracking accessible and engaging for users of all fitness levels.

### Design Process

- User research and persona development
- Wireframing and information architecture
- High-fidelity mockups and prototyping
- Usability testing and iteration
- Design system creation

### Key Features

- Personalized workout plans
- Progress tracking and analytics
- Social features for community engagement
- Integration with wearable devices
- Nutrition tracking and meal planning

### Impact

The app launched successfully with over 10,000 downloads in the first month and a 4.8-star rating on app stores.
